# AI contribution and use policy

AI tools may be used for AlbumentationsX contributions and maintenance.

Before requesting review, the author must personally read every proposed change,
understand what it does, and be able to explain the design and validation in
their own words. The author remains responsible for the code, tests,
documentation, and pull-request description.

This rule applies equally to generated code, tests, documentation, review
comments, and changes prepared by maintainers. A review performed by another
model does not replace a person's own reading and judgment.

Mentioning AI assistance in a pull request is encouraged when it helps reviewers
understand how the change was prepared, but disclosure is not a condition for
contributing. The project does not use an AI detector or require an automated
AI-specific check.
