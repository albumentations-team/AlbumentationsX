# Contribution history and relicensing boundary

This is a public audit of the evidence needed before changing AlbumentationsX's
outbound license. It does not replace contributor-specific acceptance records.

The inherited legacy Albumentations 2.0.8 material remains under the MIT notice
preserved in [`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/MIT-Albumentations-2.0.8.txt`](../../THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/MIT-Albumentations-2.0.8.txt).
AlbumentationsX began at commit `c1720fbab8209450328ef2e68f0ddc0c4806f7a8`
under AGPL version 3. Published versions `2.1.3` through `2.3.1` retain their
published `AGPL-3.0-or-later` metadata, and `2.3.2` is the first prospective
`AGPL-3.0-only` release. The full timeline and the rule against retroactive
relicensing are in [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md).

Contributor acceptance records contain personal and contractual information and
are deliberately not stored in this repository. The public history therefore
cannot prove that every contributor has granted rights for a future relicensing.
Before any such proposal, a maintainer must compare the intended affected files
and commits with the private, version-specific CLA and entity acceptance records,
and obtain any additional permissions that are missing. This review does not
alter permissions already granted for published copies.
